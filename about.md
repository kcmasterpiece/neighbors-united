  <h1>About Emeryville Neighbors United</h1>
  <p>
    Emeryville Neighbors United is a volunteer-led coalition of Emeryville and Oakland residents dedicated to
    protecting neighborhood livability, public safety, environmental quality, and transparent local
    decision-making. The organization formed in response to Sutter Health's medical campus proposal.
  </p>
  <h2>Where We Stand</h2>
  <p>
    ENU is opposed to Sutter's current plan. It is committed to keeping Emeryville and surrounding communities
    livable and safe, with streets that allow people to walk, bike, use transit, and drive safely
    and reliably. Any development at the site must provide meaningful community benefits while
    fully addressing the public costs and burdens it would place on residents. Sutter must be
    honest and transparent about its plans, and the City and community must hold it accountable.
  </p>
