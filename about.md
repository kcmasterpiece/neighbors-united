  <h1>About Neighbors United</h1>
  <p>
    Neighbors United is a volunteer-led coalition of Emeryville and Oakland residents dedicated to
    protecting neighborhood livability, public safety, environmental quality, and transparent local
    decision-making.
  </p>
  <p>
    The organization works to inform residents, encourage civic participation, and ensure that major
    development proposals are thoroughly evaluated for their long-term community impacts.
  </p>
