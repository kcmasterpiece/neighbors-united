Emeryville Neighbors United

Sutter Health’s Proposed Plan Does Not Work for Our Community

It’s too big, it’s too expensive, it’s too damaging to our community.

Sutter Health is proposing a 330-foot, 785,000-square-foot hospital as the centerpiece of a larger medical campus at 5300 Chiron Way. It would funnel an estimated 20,000 vehicle trips a day onto narrow, two-lane streets and bicycle boulevards not built to handle them. And it would increase demands on city services without providing ongoing property or business tax revenue to help close Emeryville’s $10 million deficit, projected to reach $14 million by 2030.

Act Now: Tell the City What the Environmental Review Must Study

Your opinion counts. Submit your comments on the environmental impacts of this massive development starting now. Emeryville is accepting public comments on the scope of the Environmental Impact Report for Sutter’s proposal. Review the Notice of Preparation and submit comments by 5:00 p.m. on September 14, 2026, so the EIR addresses the effects that matter to our neighborhoods.

You can also comment at the EIR scoping meeting at 6:30 p.m. on August 27, 2026, in City Council Chambers, 1333 Park Avenue.
