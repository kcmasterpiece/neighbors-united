<section class="panel">
  <p class="kicker">Key Impacts</p>
  <h1>Traffic Volume and Flow</h1>
  <ul>
    <li>Existing roads in the project area are one lane in each direction. Kaiser Oakland is the same size but is located in a larger footprint at MacArthur Boulevard and Broadway.</li>
    <li>Sutter's traffic study only covers the direct project area; the Emeryville-conducted EIR will cover 79 additional intersections, but none shown in Oakland.</li>
    <li>Sutter presentations have discussed how traffic will arrive from I-80 in Emeryville, but not from CA-24 exits in Oakland.</li>
    <li>Streets in the Emeryville project area have been recently and deliberately narrowed to prioritize bike and pedestrian traffic and transit.</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="panel">
  <h2>Parking</h2>
  <p>Garage parking will be paid, and current Alta Bates patients have complained about the costs.</p>
  <p>Nearly all adjacent streets in Emeryville and Oakland have unrestricted free parking.</p>
  <ul>
    <li>People going to the medical center will overwhelm street parking and local residential lots.</li>
    <li>Unattended parked cars are targets for crime that can spill over to residences.</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="panel">
  <h2>Public Safety</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>The Emeryville Police Department and Alameda County Fire Department Emeryville District are already understaffed.</li>
    <li>The neighborhood already experiences mail theft, bike theft, abandoned stolen vehicles, and car theft.</li>
    <li>Introducing 12,000 additional people per day to the neighborhood will strain emergency services.</li>
  </ul>
</section>
